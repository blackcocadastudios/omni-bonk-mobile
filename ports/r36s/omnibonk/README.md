# Omni Bonk

**Ready to Run** PortMaster-style port for the Anbernic R36S and similar RK3326 handhelds (aarch64, SDL2, OpenGL ES 3.0).
No game files to copy and nothing to buy: everything is in the package.

> **Omni Bonk is a non-commercial fan project made in honor of the success of [Megabonk](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3405340/Megabonk/) by vedinad.**
> It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or derived from the original game's files. All models, textures, icons, sounds and music
> are original and generated by code in the same low-poly / PS1 style.

## Premise

A 3D low-poly roguelite survivor. You are dropped into a map, your attacks fire on their own, and you survive waves of enemies
while you run, jump and slide. Level up, pick weapons, tomes and items, open chests, fight bosses and escape through the portal
before the Final Swarm gets you.

- 5 characters, 3 maps (Forest, Desert, Graveyard) with 3 tiers each
- 43 enemies and bosses, 30 weapons, 23 tomes, 86 items, shrines, shops and upgrade towers
- Adjustable time per stage (default **15 minutes**; the original uses 10), optional Final Swarm, DPS meter, chest counter, auto bunny hop
- 6 languages: English (default), Portuguese (BR), Spanish, French, Russian, German (Settings > Game > Language)
- Procedural art, music and sound effects: tiny download, no external assets

## Installation

1. Put the SD card with your games in a PC (the `EASYROMS` partition on dArkOS / ArkOS).
2. Extract the zip into the `ports` folder so you end up with:
   ```
   ports/
   ├── OmniBonk.sh
   └── omnibonk/
       ├── omnibonk        (game binary, aarch64)
       ├── port.json, gameinfo.xml, README.md, screenshot.png
       └── conf/           (created on first run: save file and settings)
   ```
3. Put the card back in the console, then *Start > Quit > Restart EmulationStation*.
4. Launch **Omni Bonk** from the *Ports* system.

PortMaster users can also drop the zip into `ports/autoinstall` (or install it from the PortMaster app).

**Requirements:** a 64-bit ARM handheld CFW with SDL2 and OpenGL ES 3.0 drivers (dArkOS, ArkOS, ROCKNIX, AmberELEC and similar).
Developed and tested on the R36S with 1 GB of RAM (see Performance). Reports from other devices are welcome.

## Performance

Tested on an **R36S with 1 GB of RAM** (RK3326, dArkOS, March 2026 release):

| Mode | Settings | Measured FPS |
|---|---|---|
| **Auto quality** (default) | Defaults below, adjusted on the fly | **40 - 60 FPS**, consistently |
| Heaviest | Shadows on, particles high, max enemies, render scale 75% | 24 - 30 FPS |

The handheld defaults are: render scale 75%, up to 220 enemies, medium particles, view distance 90 m, vibration off.

### Auto quality mode

*Auto quality* (Settings > Graphics, **on by default**) watches the real frame rate while you play and lowers the cost of the game
by itself, so you never have to dig through the menus:

1. It measures the average FPS over 5-second windows, ignoring the first 4 seconds of every stage (loading hitches).
2. The target is your FPS limit, capped at 60 (Settings > Graphics > FPS limit). If the average falls below **72% of the target**
   (about 43 FPS with a 60 FPS limit, about 22 FPS with a 30 FPS limit), it takes **one step down**, shows a short on-screen banner
   with the new value, and measures again 5 seconds later. The steps, in order:
   1. **Render scale** drops 10% at a time, down to a minimum of 50% (the picture gets a bit more pixelated)
   2. **Particles** go down one level (high > medium > low)
   3. **View distance** goes to 90 m, then 70 m
   4. **Maximum enemies** goes to 220, then 150
3. Once the frame rate is back above the threshold it stops. It only ever lowers quality, never raises it again, and the result is
   saved, so the next run starts already adjusted.

To go back to the full-quality settings, turn Auto quality off and set the values you want in Settings > Graphics (or delete
`omnibonk/conf/omnibonk_save.txt`). You can also show the FPS counter there, or cap the frame rate at 30 for steadier pacing.

## Troubleshooting

`omnibonk/log.txt` is rewritten on every launch. It records the firmware, the video driver, the controller mapping and the FPS every
10 seconds. If something is wrong (black screen, swapped buttons, low FPS), please attach that file to your report.
The save file is `omnibonk/conf/omnibonk_save.txt` (delete it to reset everything).
